# features/sector

SeamAssembler / KitAssembly / anchors (TECH-001 §2.2–§2.3), and the sector camera (TECH-001 §2.5). Owner docs: ZONES-001 §1.7, §4; camera numbers SYS-001 §9. The bootstrap stub lives in scenes/sector/.

Status: camera built (M1 slice 3 PR 1): `sector_camera.gd` (`SectorCamera`, a `Camera2D` stepped in the physics tick) and `sector_camera_stats.gd` (`SectorCameraStats`, data in `data/sector/sector_camera.tres`, `ds_camera.*`, TECH-001 §3.4). Torus sandbox (Q-T58): `sector_wrap.gd` (`SectorWrap`, wraps the Vigil after its physics step and keeps other actors on the image nearest it), the camera follows by `Torus.delta` and rebases at the seam. Sector slice look (2026-11-19): the M1 debug grid (`SectorFloor`, `SectorFloorStyle`) is retired (TECH-001 Q-T163 resolved); `sector_depth.gd` (`SectorDepth`) with `sector_depth_style.gd` (`SectorDepthStyle`, data `data/sector/sector_depth_back.tres` and `sector_depth_front.tres`, ✱ visual values) draws the near depth (fog wisps, motes) around the play layer; `race_framer.gd` (`RaceFramer`) leans the camera toward the race through `SectorCamera.frame_offset` (`ds_camera.race_*`, proposed in SYS-001 §9; zoom stays fixed, TECH-001 §2.5). Open: Q-T211. Tests in `tests/` (`test_race_framer.gd` added). SeamAssembler, KitAssembly and anchors are not built. Follow docs/04-production/ADDING_A_FEATURE.md before adding code.
